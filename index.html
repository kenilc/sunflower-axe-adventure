FIELD NOTES

Small hero. Big axe.

Explore the garden as our flower-hooded adventurer. Collect all 8 sunstones, smash 12 targets, then return to the glowing shrine. Follow the north path beyond the shrine to enter the treasure cave. Inside, walk back through the blue-lit south passage to leave. Take the flower-covered gate on the west path to Rainbow Riverside. Walk up to 18 colourful gems and stones hidden in six clusters near the riverside landmarks to collect them, cross the wooden bridge, and return through the gate beside the rainbow lookout. A forested mountain waterfall feeds the riverside. Take the boat to Lotus Lagoon, then cross the bridge to the flower island and ride the cable car to the mountain summit. Enter the castle arch to discover a furnished great room: find six hidden stars, unlock the chest, share tea, play piano, read the storybook, and lie down together on the bed. Use X or Get up to finish resting. Leave through the pink arch to return to the summit.

Follow the sunflower gate on the east path to Sunflower Funfair. Ride the Ferris wheel and woodland carousel together. Press X near a ride to board, or again to finish early. At the southeast ring-toss booth, press X to play, then time another X when the moving marker reaches the green zone. Win three plush prizes! Return through the south gate.

Continue through the funfair's north lantern gate to a Japanese summer night festival. Both friends change into t-shirts and shorts. Explore the food stalls and riverside tea house, and find fireflies in the gardens. Walk onto the central bridge to stop together: the camera shows your happy faces, then tilts up to the fireworks. Press X to finish early or replay the moment. Return through the south lantern gate.

Take the chalet gate on the garden's south path to Edelweiss Village. Enter the bakery, outfit shop and florist with X near their doors. Visit the counters to share pastries, try matching alpine scarves, and pick a bouquet. Pet the eight sheep in the eastern meadow with X. The west path follows narrow ledges along the mountain face, climbing iron-rung ascents and crossing a gorge on a cable bridge. Follow it to the summit and press X to sit together in the mountain cart. Raise your arms and enjoy a close-up ride down the trail to the village. Harnesses appear for both hikers on the ascent. Walk through the village's south gate to reach the garden.
