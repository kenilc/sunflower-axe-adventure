# Journey storyboard — _Following the Seed_

Draft for review. Nothing here is built yet.

## The thread

The friends sit under the big garden sunflower when the wind takes its last seed. It drifts over the wall and they follow it. The seed shows up at every stop, caught in a windmill sail, floating in a paper lantern on a misty pond and so on, until they plant it in Sunflower Valley. Back in the garden, a young sunflower now stands where they first sat.

- **Start.** A new **Seed Gate** stands in the garden's northwest corner. A small wooden sign shows a seed with a dotted line.
- **Order.** Each stop opens the next. You travel on that stop's own vehicle, the same way the boat and cable car work. Once a stop is visited, Map / V can bring you back to it.
- **Map.** A dotted **trail** winds across the map. Stops you haven't reached stay under mist, and the dotted line grows one stop at a time.
- **Passport.** A new Keepsakes tab. Each stop gives one illustrated stamp and one postcard, and the postcard is the place's signature photo spot (marked ★). The final page fills in once the seed is planted.
- **The seed.** At every stop it's a small floating glint with a soft glow. Finding it opens the way onward. It's never hidden behind anything, just a little out of the way.

## Atmosphere rules (keep what the game already feels like)

- Cosy, unhurried and wordless. There are no fail states, timers or scores. Activities are done together and stop with **X**.
- Every stop gets its own **diorama board**, its own sky, sun and fog settings, and its own melody.
- Each stop changes from the one before in **at least two** of time of day, weather and biome.
- **Outfits** change on arrival, the way the beach and winter village do it now, and switch back when you leave.
- The camera eases closer at photo spots and pulls back on the paths, like the village does.

## Photo spots

Every stop has **six or more photo spots**: views and props set up so a shot already looks good. Each has:

- a **framing prop**, such as an arch, a window, a bench or a frame that leans on something
- a short **pose** both friends take when you press X nearby (sit, lean, wave or hold hands), which Camera / M can then pause
- an album caption, like the activity photos already have

★ marks the postcard shot. When you take a photo at a spot, it gets a small paw-print tick in the passport. This is optional and nothing is locked behind it.

Legend for the board sketches: `@` arrival, `S` seed, `→` exit or vehicle, `1–8` photo spots, `★` postcard.

## 1 · Tulip Polder

_Spring morning in Dutch flatland. A breeze, birdsong, and the sound of windmill sails._

|             |                                                                                          |

| Board       | Square, about 34 half-width. Grass `#7fae5a`, peat soil `#5e4634`, clay stone `#a88f75`  |
| Music       | Light accordion and a music-box waltz                                                    |
| Outfit      | Striped shirts, rolled trousers, wooden-soled shoes that click on the dike               |
| Arrive      | Over a small bridge from the Seed Gate                                                   |

```
   ~~~~~~~~ canal ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ → balloon (5)
   ===== dike path ==========================
   |red|yel|pink|red|  ⌂ windmill 3★  |pur|
   |red|yel|pink|red|      S          |pur|
   ===== dike path ==========================
   2 bike shed   4 drawbridge   6 cheese cart

**Beats**

1. You arrive among rows of tulips, red, yellow, pink and purple, the colours of a flag. Bikes lean on the fence.
2. **Tandem bike:** press X at the shed and both friends pedal along the dike loop. The tulips rush past, and a petal trail follows the bike.
3. **Windmill:** the seed is stuck on a sail. The friends turn the cap together (a short push), the sails start, and the seed drops loose.
4. **Feed the ducks** at the canal edge (an extra moment).
5. Leave by the **striped balloon** waiting on the canal bank.

**Photo spots**

1. Tulip bench: sit between the colour strips.
2. Leaning on bikes at the shed.
3. ★ Holding hands on the dike, the windmill behind with its sails mid-turn.
4. On the white drawbridge, looking down the canal.
5. Waving from the balloon basket as it lifts off over the tulips.
6. Sharing a wheel of cheese at the cheese cart.

Stamp: a red tulip on a windmill.

        1 meadow (fawn)            → balloon (9) ≈

```

**Beats**

1. Follow the paper lanterns north up a winding stone path through the fog.
2. **Mist puffs:** in a glade beside the path, soft round wisps with tiny faces come to say hello and float round the friends.
3. **The houses:** a frog, a cat, a rabbit, an owl and a turtle in its shell sit round a little pond. Each is big enough to walk into, with a round room inside that suits its animal. The animal is at home, a little storybook figure in its own clothes: the frog in a raincoat fishes from its lily pad, the cat in a cardigan knits a scarf, the rabbit in an apron nibbles a carrot, the owl in a vest reads in its armchair and the turtle in a scarf sips tea. Each looks up when the friends come near. Each house gives a happy wiggle as you step back out.
4. **Paper lanterns:** set lanterns floating on the pond. The seed is resting in one of them.
5. **Forest corners:** a trail west from the clearing leads to a grove of tall soft-capped toadstools round a fairy ring of glowing mushrooms; a trail east crosses a stream on a little humped bridge to a berry patch with a basket left on a stump.
6. **Forest animals:** five storybook animals stroll the hollow, upright in their own clothes like the house residents: a hedgehog in lavender dungarees and a beanie with a basket of toadstools (the grove), a squirrel in a moss hoodie with an acorn (the berry patch), a fox in a cream turtleneck with a paper lantern (over the bridge), a fawn in a rose poncho and daisy crown (the meadow and lantern path) and a duck in a coral-striped top under a lilac umbrella (round the pond). When the friends come near they keep strolling, only slower, stepping round them and glancing over now and then. Press X by one and it comes to stand beside them and waves, three-quarters to the camera, and he steps to her other side; Camera / M frames all three.
7. **Rest** on the mossy log by the pond and watch the mist, then take the balloon on from the meadow.

**Photo spots**

1. Under the first paper lantern at the meadow's edge.
2. ★ On the stepping stones before the cat house, all five houses round the pond in the mist.
3. Among the mist puffs in the glade.
4. At the pond's edge with lanterns drifting on the water.
5. Side by side on the mossy log.
6. By the frog's indoor pool, under the firefly jars.
7. On the owl's reading rug, under the star garland.
8. At the turtle's low tea table.
9. In the balloon basket, rising out of the mist.
10. Under the toadstools in the grove, with the hedgehog waving.
11. Among the berry bushes, with the squirrel and its acorn.

Stamp: a sleepy cat-house face with a lantern.

## Open questions for review

- Is the seed gate in the **northwest** of the garden OK? It needs a clear spot.
- Should the trip also unlock the existing places in some order, or leave them as side trips off the garden?
- Should the night train be a moving room, or a still carriage with scrolling window scenes only?
- Is it OK for photo-spot ticks to count toward something, such as a badge on the passport cover? Or should they stay purely decorative?
- Should outfit changes happen at every stop, or only where the weather calls for it (rain, desert, autumn)?
